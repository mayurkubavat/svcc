Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "svcc: SystemVerilog-to-SystemC Conversion with a Formal Check of the Generated C++", for publication in SoftwareX as an Original Software Publication.

svcc is an open-source Rust tool, with a Python package, that converts synthesizable SystemVerilog RTL into readable SystemC and then checks the generated model formally. The check reads the generated C++ with C++17 and SystemC evaluation rules and proves it equivalent to the SystemVerilog source with the Z3 solver. A second command simulates the generated SystemC against Verilator. Version 0.1.0 is released under the Apache 2.0 license on crates.io and PyPI, and the repository holds every script and result the manuscript refers to.

Hardware design and verification teams keep SystemC models of their RTL for virtual platforms, reference models and simulation without a commercial license. These models are mostly written by hand, and a wrong model often compiles and passes review. I am not aware of another open-source SystemVerilog-to-SystemC converter that checks its output against the source.

A companion manuscript, "Translation Validation of SystemVerilog-to-SystemC RTL Conversion by Reading the Generated C++", describes the check method, an earlier version of it that failed, and its evaluation. It is being posted as an arXiv preprint and has not been submitted to a journal. The two manuscripts cite each other. This submission describes the software, its architecture, its use and its limits, and does not repeat the companion paper's evaluation.
